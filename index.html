Hi, I'm Fengshuo Chen (陈烽硕), an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Hangzhou Dianzi University, admitted to the Master's program in Electronic Information at Fudan University.

My research focuses on multimodal visual understanding, especially salient object detection and few-shot semantic segmentation. I explore how modality quality, language semantics, and efficient architectures can improve vision models in complex imaging scenarios.

Text-Image Guided Retrieval Network for Triple-Modal Images Few-Shot Semantic Segmentation

Mamba-Based Lightweight Multimodal Salient Object Detection
